# Bearcat Study Room Portal

**University of Cincinnati | Senior Design 2026–2027 | Team 50**

## Overview

The Bearcat Study Room Portal is a web application designed to simplify the process of finding and reserving study rooms at the University of Cincinnati's Langsam Library.

The project aims to improve upon the current reservation experience by providing a more straightforward way for students to check room availability and make reservations. Rather than navigating through multiple searches and pages, students will be able to view available rooms on an interactive map and reserve a space with minimal steps.

The application will also include a live display inside Langsam Library, allowing students to check room reservation availability when they enter the building.

## Project Goals

Our team aims to:

- Simplify the process of finding and reserving study rooms.
- Make room availability the main focus of the application.
- Reduce the number of steps required to complete a reservation.
- Provide a visual layout of study rooms for easier selection.
- Allow students to find rooms that meet their specific needs.
- Improve accessibility and usability across different devices.
- Protect student privacy by limiting the reservation information visible to others.

## Planned Features

### Interactive Room Map

A visual map of study rooms on each floor of Langsam Library. Rooms will be color-coded to indicate their reservation status, making it easier to identify available spaces.

### Simplified Room Reservations

Students will be able to select a room directly from the map. A reservation popup will automatically fill in relevant room information to minimize the number of steps required to book.

### Current and Future Availability

Students will be able to check which rooms are currently available and view availability for future dates and times.

### Room Filtering

Students will be able to find rooms based on specific accommodations, including available audio/visual equipment.

### Reservation Management

Students will be able to view, update, and cancel existing reservations.

### Email Confirmations

Students will receive an email confirming their reservation after successfully booking a study room.

### Live Library Display

A physical display in Langsam Library will provide an updated overview of room reservation availability using a layout consistent with the online application.

### QR Code Reservations

Students will be able to scan QR codes associated with study rooms to access reservation information and begin the booking process.

## Design Considerations

### Accessibility

The application will be designed with accessibility in mind so students can navigate the system and reserve study rooms regardless of their abilities.

### Privacy

Room availability will be visible to students without exposing other users' personal information or reservation details.

### Usability

The interface will prioritize clear navigation, minimal steps, and an easy-to-understand reservation process.
